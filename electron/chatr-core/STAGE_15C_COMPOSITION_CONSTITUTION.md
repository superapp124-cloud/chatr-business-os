# STAGE 15C: The Composition Constitution
## Universal Composition Framework
**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Ratified

---

## Mission
Stages 1–14 established the Platform Mechanics.
Stage 15A established the Semantic Constitution.
Stage 15B established the Business Runtime.
Stage 15C establishes how the platform evolves forever through **composable business solutions**.

This constitution defines the immutable rules for extending the CHATR Intent Operating System without fragmenting the core architecture.

---

## 1. Composition Over Modification
Nothing modifies the Kernel, the frozen ABI contracts, the Canonical Ontology, or the Business Runtime. Every Industry, Enterprise, AI, Experience, or Regional Solution is a **Composition Package**.

## 2. Extension Before Replacement
Every Solution Pack extends canonical behavior; nothing replaces canonical behavior. If a capability exists, it must be reused.

## 3. Capability Reuse First
Semantic reuse is mandatory. A new capability may only be created when semantic reuse is impossible. Redundant capabilities that perform the same business verb on the same canonical object will be rejected during certification.

## 4. Canonical Object Integrity
Solution Packs may add:
- Attributes
- Relationships
- Capabilities
- Policies
- Templates
- Providers
But they may **never redefine** canonical meaning established in Stage 15A.

## 5. Provider Independence
Solutions never bind to specific vendors. Required providers are explicitly declared in the manifest, but provider resolution always remains a runtime decision governed by the tenant's policies.

## 6. Intelligence Compatibility
Every extension automatically participates in the Intelligence Plane. By conforming to the Business Runtime intelligence contract, every capability automatically feeds the Learning Engine and Recommendation systems.

## 7. Marketplace Compatibility
Every extension must be packageable according to the Exchange Plane contract (Stage 12). They must be portable, signed, and certifiable.

## 8. Federation Ready
Every extension explicitly declares its federation behavior, ensuring trust boundaries (Stage 14) are never silently bypassed.

---

## The Composition Decision Record (CDR)
To govern the massive ecosystem of solutions, we introduce the **Composition Decision Record (CDR)**.
- **ADR** governs Architecture (The OS).
- **ODR** governs Ontology (The Meaning).
- **CDR** governs Composition (The Solutions).

Any major cross-domain dependency, new solution template, or cross-pack integration standard must be documented and ratified as a CDR.
